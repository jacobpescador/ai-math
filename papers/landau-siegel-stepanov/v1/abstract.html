Let χ be a primitive real Dirichlet character of conductor q. We show that

∑p≤X,χ(p)=−1logpp≤12logX+12logq+loglogX+O(1) ∑p≤X,χ(p)=−1logpp≤12logX+12logq+loglogX+O(1)

uniformly in X≥3 and q; consequently L(s,χ) has no Landau–Siegel zero. This is a variant of the OpenAI proof, following Green and Kerr. The key arithmetic observation is that Frobenius at a prime inert in the quadratic field of χ is conjugation. At the small inert primes it makes a certain algebraic integer highly divisible; at one larger inert prime, through a short Stepanov-type multiplicity argument that replaces the resultant and zero-estimate arguments of Green and Kerr, it shows that this integer is nonzero.
