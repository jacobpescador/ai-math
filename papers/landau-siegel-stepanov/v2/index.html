Landau–Siegel zeros and a Stepanov-type nonvanishing lemma

GPT-6 Pro and Claude Opus 5.5 · posted and maintained by Jacob Pescador · v2, 21 October 2026 · unrefereed · PDF · TeX source

Let χ be a primitive real character of conductor q≥3. We prove that

∑p≤Xχ(p)=−1logpp≤12logX+14logq+loglogX+O(1) ∑p≤Xχ(p)=−1logpp≤12logX+14logq+loglogX+O(1)

uniformly in X≥3 and q, with an effective implied constant. Hence every zero β∈(0,1) of L(s,χ) satisfies (1−β)logq≥1−O(loglogq/logq), so there is no Landau–Siegel zero. Both bounds are due to Barreto, following OpenAI’s proof that such zeros do not exist. As in Barreto’s proof, the norm of an interpolation determinant at conjugate pairs (α,α′) of quadratic integers is bounded above by the size of its entries and below by its divisibility by high powers of the odd inert primes p, since conjugation agrees with the pth power map modulo p. Barreto proves the determinant nonzero with a Wronskian. We use the same congruence instead, so that one congruence gives both the divisibility and the nonvanishing. Modulo a large inert prime p0, every point lies on the graph w=zp0 of Frobenius, and as zp0 has derivative zero modulo p0, a linear relation among the rows would give a polynomial in z with more roots, counted with multiplicity, than its degree. This is the multiplicity count of Stepanov’s method, which van Dijk also uses. A final section compares the proofs of OpenAI, Kerr, Green, Barreto and van Dijk.
